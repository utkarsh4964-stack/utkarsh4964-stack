### 🧭 About Me

I'm an Information Technology undergrad at **JSS Academy of Technical Education, Noida** (Class of 2028), currently working as an **AI Developer Intern at Good Health 24/7**, and building my own AI startup on the side. I ship end-to-end — backend architecture, LLM orchestration, and deployment — solo.

- 🔭 Currently building **Checkpoint** — a reliability & audit layer ("Git for AI agent actions") for autonomous AI agents
- ⚖️ Building **AI Case Report** — an AI-powered legal case investigation platform with entity graphs, RBAC, and document hashing
- 📄 Author of an **arXiv research paper** on Explainable AI (XAI) applied to credit scoring
- 🧠 Deep interest in **multi-agent systems, agentic infrastructure, and explainable fintech AI**
- 🚀 Ships fast: most projects go from idea → deployed product on Railway / Render / Vercel within days
- 🏆 SIH2024 finalist, active hackathon builder (HackAgentAIx and more)
